Bitmesh: Offline Chat

Talk with no signal.

Bitmesh keeps your group talking when the network doesn't. No account. No phone number.

Coming soon to the App Store

01

Phone to phone

Messages hop between nearby phones over Bluetooth. They also use the internet when it is there.

02

Crews

Small private groups of up to 16 people. End-to-end encrypted, so only the group can read them.

03

Rooms

Talk to everyone nearby, or join a room for this venue, this area or this city.

Made for festivals, events, travel and places where phones stop working.

The short version

  • No account, phone number or email needed.
  • The developer collects no data about you. No analytics, ads or tracking.
  • Compatible with the open Bitchat protocol.
  • Public rooms are public. Private chats and Crews are encrypted.
